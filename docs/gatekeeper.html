Installation & Security Guide

Thank you for downloading Thumbnail Workbench! Because the app is independently distributed, your operating system may trigger a routine first-time security prompt. Here is how to open it instantly:

macOS (Gatekeeper Bypass)

  1. Open your Applications folder.
  2. Hold down the Control key and click (or right-click) the Thumbnail Workbench icon.
  3. Choose Open from the menu, then click Open on the warning dialog.

Windows (SmartScreen Notice)

  1. If Windows Defender shows a blue "Protected your PC" popup, click the "More info" text link.
  2. Click the "Run anyway" button that appears at the bottom.

You only need to complete this one-time step for your initial launch!
