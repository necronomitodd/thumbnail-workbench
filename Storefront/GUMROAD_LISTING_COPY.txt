Stop fighting bloated photo editors just to frame, test, and export a clean video thumbnail.

**Thumbnail Workbench** is a fast, lightweight, single-serving desktop application built with Rust and Tauri. It launches instantaneously, uses less than 15 MB of disk space, and runs 100% locally on your computer with zero subscriptions, cloud logins, or telemetry.

### What's Included in Your Download:
* **macOS Universal Installer (`thumbnail-workbench_0.1.0_universal.dmg`):** Runs natively on both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.
* **Windows 10/11 Setup Wizard (`thumbnail-workbench_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`):** Standard Windows installer with Desktop & Start Menu shortcuts.
* **Windows Portable Executable (`thumbnail-workbench_0.1.0_x64-portable.exe`):** Zero-install standalone version—just double-click and run!
* **Quick Installation Guide (`README_FIRST_INSTALLATION_GUIDE.txt`):** Simple 10-second instructions for opening independent apps on macOS and Windows.
